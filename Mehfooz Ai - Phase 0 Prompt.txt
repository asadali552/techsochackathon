## 1. UNDERSTAND THE COMPLETE PRODUCT VISION

### What is Mehfooz Ai?

Mehfooz Ai is a community-powered geographical safety intelligence and travel planning platform.

The core problem:

When people travel through unfamiliar cities, neighbourhoods or tourist destinations, they often lack reliable information about:

- Whether an area has recently experienced reported crime.
- Whether particular routes have official safety warnings.
- Whether there are recent incidents of robbery, harassment, snatching or accidents.
- How travel conditions differ depending on the time of day.
- Whether environmental factors or road hazards could affect their journey.
- What alternative routes are available.
- What safety precautions they should consider.

Mehfooz Ai combines community reports, available official advisories, geographical information and AI-based analysis to help users make more informed travel decisions.

Our objective is not to guarantee safety or predict individual victimization. Our objective is to communicate available safety information, reported incidents, risks and uncertainty in an accessible way.

### The product has five core subsystems.

**SYSTEM A — DYNAMIC SAFETY HEATMAP**

The interactive map is the central component of the entire application.

It should visualize geographical safety information using:

- Green: relatively lower observed risk according to available data.
- Yellow: moderate reported risk.
- Red: elevated reported risk.
- Grey: insufficient information.

The system must never interpret missing reports as proof of safety.

The heatmap must support:

1. Current reported-risk visualization.
2. Historical time-of-day visualization.
3. Community incident overlays.
4. Official restricted/unsafe route overlays.
5. Geographic exploration.
6. Dynamic updates when new reports are submitted.

Official restrictions must remain independently identifiable and should not be diluted by unrelated traffic indicators.

**SYSTEM B — COMMUNITY INCIDENT REPORTING**

Users can report safety-related problems, including:

- Robbery.
- Theft and mobile snatching.
- Harassment.
- Suspicious activity.
- Road accidents.
- Poor street lighting.
- Dangerous road conditions.
- Landslides and other travel hazards.

Each report should contain:

- Incident type/category.
- Description.
- Geographic location.
- Incident date/time.
- Optional evidence.
- Verification status.

New reports should appear as markers and contribute appropriately to the geographical intelligence engine.

Reports must be moderated or labelled as unverified until appropriate corroboration.

**SYSTEM C — AI SAFETY INTELLIGENCE ENGINE**

AI is not merely a chatbot.

It must provide meaningful processing capabilities:

1. Incident description classification.
2. Structured extraction of incident information.
3. Provisional severity estimation.
4. Duplicate-report detection.
5. Support for report credibility assessment.
6. Explanation of geographical incident patterns.

Use deterministic, explainable calculations for geographical scoring.

Do not allow language models to invent incident statistics or make unsupported safety claims.

**SYSTEM D — SAFETY-AWARE ROUTE PLANNING**

Users can specify:

- Starting location.
- Destination.
- Departure time.
- Travel preferences.

The system should:

1. Retrieve alternative road routes.
2. Identify available incident data near each route.
3. Check relevant official restrictions.
4. Evaluate road hazards separately from security-related incidents.
5. Compare routes using transparent scoring.
6. Display route alternatives on the map.
7. Explain the trade-offs between distance, estimated travel time and available safety evidence.

Never label a route as guaranteed safe.

**SYSTEM E — AGENTIC AI TRAVEL ASSISTANT**

Implement an AI assistant capable of using actual backend tools.

Potential user requests:

- "Is this area safe to visit tonight?"
- "Show recent incidents near my location."
- "Plan a trip from Multan to Lahore."
- "Which route has fewer reported incidents?"
- "Help me plan a family trip to Hunza."
- "Are there any reported road hazards on this route?"

The assistant must retrieve available data using tool calling.

Proposed tools:

- get_area_safety
- get_nearby_incidents
- get_route_options
- compare_route_risks
- get_official_advisories
- get_weather_conditions
- find_emergency_facilities

Only expose tools once a working underlying implementation or data source exists.

Unavailable data must be clearly identified rather than fabricated.

### Future expansion

The architecture should support tourism use cases across Pakistan, including Hunza, Skardu, Naran and other northern areas.

Potential future features:

- Road closure alerts.
- Weather-related travel intelligence.
- Landslide information.
- Emergency assistance.
- Government integrations.
- Community verification.
- Push notifications.

Do not overengineer these future features during the hackathon.
