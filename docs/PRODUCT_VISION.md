# Product vision

MehfoozAI helps people exploring unfamiliar areas see where safety concerns have been reported, when reports occurred, and which sources support them. The pilot serves local commuters, students, families, and visitors in Multan.

| Subsystem | Pilot experience |
| --- | --- |
| Safety heatmap | Explore current and historical time windows with incident markers and independently visible official overlays. Refresh after eligible reports change. |
| Community reporting | Submit a category, description, location, and time. Show review status. Defer evidence uploads until private storage and review are available. |
| AI safety intelligence | Classify reports, extract structured facts, propose severity, and flag likely duplicates for review. Summarize evidence from backend queries. |
| Route planning | Compare available alternatives by time, distance, nearby security reports, road hazards, official restrictions, and evidence coverage. |
| Travel assistant | Answer area, route, and advisory questions through available backend tools and show source and freshness information. |

Conventional directions primarily describe how to get somewhere. MehfoozAI adds local evidence, source provenance, and uncertainty to route choices. Green describes relatively lower observed risk given sufficient evidence. Grey describes insufficient information. No band guarantees a safe trip.

Limit the MVP to a small Multan pilot with a clearly isolated fictional demonstration if real data are sparse. A verified feed is required before the app can report official restrictions as known. Future tourism coverage may include Hunza, Skardu, and Naran with road closures, weather, landslides, and emergency facilities where sources exist.
